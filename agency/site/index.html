FAQ

Straight answers

How long until SEO actually brings in leads?

A new site converts existing traffic immediately. Ranking improvements typically show in 2–3 months for suburb-level terms and 4–6+ months for competitive city-wide terms. We report progress monthly so you always know where you stand.

Do I own my website?

Yes — the domain, the site, the content, all of it. If you ever leave, we hand over everything and help you move. No hostage-taking.

Will you work with my competitor down the road?

No. One business per trade, per region. That exclusivity is part of what you're paying for.

We get all our work from word of mouth. Why bother?

Referrals are gold — but nearly every referral still Googles you before calling. A weak site loses referred leads you never knew you had, and rankings smooth out the quiet months.

What do you need from me?

About an hour of your time up front, photos of your best installs, and quick answers when we check trade details. We handle the rest — copy, build, Google, tracking.
